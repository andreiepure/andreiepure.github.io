<h1>About</h1>

I'm a Senior Staff Software Engineer at [Sonar](https://www.sonarsource.com/). I'm a technical leader with experience leading both as an individual contributor and as an engineering manager.

After three years as an Engineering Manager, I've moved back to hands-on development at Sonar. The move came down to two things: the chance to deepen my skills in cloud development and distributed systems design, and the belief that agentic development has changed what's possible for individual contributors to accomplish.

## How I work

I have a strong technical voice. I like to read the code and understand how our systems work. I also care about how our customers are using the product. I aim to bring clarity in ambiguous cross-functional, ever-changing environments.

I care about observability, production investigation, and business telemetry. When something goes wrong, I want us to know quickly and understand what happened. I expect every engineer in the team to have a learning mindset.

I've navigated both flat organisations and more hierarchical ones. I believe in giving engineers trust, ownership and opportunities to grow. And I am there to support when things get complicated.
